# Starlight Canvas

The director's room on the Starlight substrate.

One infinite graph. Three houses. Four memory planes.

| House | What it directs |
| --- | --- |
| Starlight | Agent OS. The contract every other house runs on. |
| Arcanea | Worlds, books, mythology. A creative director who keeps the bible. |
| GenCreator | Series and modern socials. A showrunner who ships one shape per place. |

This repository is the constitution: the bench, the skill, the refusal, and the three passes that come next. The interactive room — pan, wire, run, one Grok pass per node — is the Starlight Canvas studio.

## Planes

- **Working** dies with the task.
- **Episodic** is the run log.
- **World** is canon. Eval before write.
- **Audience** is who it is for, and it never rewrites the world.

## What is honest

- The local runtime walks the graph in order and writes a beat in that seat's voice. No quota.
- Grok rewrites a single node, and only when a director asks.
- Lens nodes are Higgsfield job tickets. They do not pretend a clip exists.
- Seats, Stripe, and a skill exchange are designed. They are not charged yet.
- The bench (Iri, Sable, Noor, Jun, Kepler, Mercer, Harbor) are seats we would hire. They are not real people and not endorsements.
